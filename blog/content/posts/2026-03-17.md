## Systems for multi-model and multi-agent inference

- ICaRus (Identical Cache Reuse) decomposes decoder-only Transformers into a frozen “logical encoder” and fine-tuned “logical decoder” so multiple task-specialized models can fully share KV caches layer-wise for identical prompts, enabling cross-model prefix caching and parallel KV generation+decoding via adapters like LoRA; in vLLM-based multi-agent workflows with eight models, it matches task-specific accuracy while cutting P95 latency up to 11.1× and boosting throughput up to 3.8×. ([arXiv: ICaRus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13281))  
- RelayCaching is a training-free inference technique that reuses decoding-phase KV caches from upstream agents as prefill caches for downstream agents despite differing prefixes, selectively recomputing only sparse, deviation-heavy positions (identified via layerwise value cosine similarity) and achieving over 80% KV reuse, up to 4.7× lower time-to-first-token, and negligible accuracy loss on collaborative tasks in math, knowledge, and code generation. ([arXiv: RelayCaching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13289))  
- Empirical work on “instruction fade-out” shows that, even when a system prompt remains in context, its influence decays as conversations lengthen—Nemotron‑3 Super obeyed a strict JSON-output instruction perfectly for six turns but began drifting by turn 7 and 14—while injecting short event-driven system reminders every few turns restored mid-conversation compliance (from 90% to 95%) and highlights that prompt persistence must be treated as an architectural concern of the agent harness, not just prompt engineering. ([Cobus Greyling](https://cobusgreyling.substack.com/p/instruction-fade-out-is-the-silent))  

## Knowledge graphs, retrieval, and reasoning

- EMBRAG combines LLM-generated logic rules with embedding-space reasoning over knowledge graphs: it first mines multiple candidate rules via random walks, scores them per question cluster, applies them as constraints in embedding space rather than strict graph traversal to overcome KG incompleteness, and then uses an LLM reranker to interpret and refine results, achieving new SOTA performance on two KGQA benchmarks and reducing hallucinations. ([arXiv: EMBRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13266))  
- INSES (Intelligent Navigation and Similarity Enhanced Search) targets GraphRAG over noisy/sparse KGs by coupling LLM-guided navigation—which prunes irrelevant neighbors and steers traversal—with embedding-based similarity expansion that dynamically creates “virtual edges” between semantically close nodes, plus a router that sends simple queries to naive RAG and complex ones to INSES, yielding accuracy gains of 5–27% over SOTA RAG/GraphRAG on the MINE benchmark across KGs built by KGGEN, GraphRAG, and OpenIE. ([arXiv: INSES](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14006))  
- Moonshot’s Attention Residuals architecture replaces fixed residual accumulation with input-dependent attention over prior layers, validated at scale on a 48B total/3B-active Kimi Linear model with claimed 1.25× compute advantage and <2% latency overhead, sparking debate over novelty versus prior work but illustrating the growing importance of architectural innovation that’s proven at frontier scale. ([Latent.Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-nvidia-gtc-jensen-goes-hard))  

## Security, fraud, and blockchain analysis

- A proposed dual-path generative framework for bank fraud detection pairs a low-latency VAE that learns a manifold of legitimate transactions (using reconstruction error to flag anomalies in <50 ms) with an asynchronous WGAN‑GP that synthesizes high-entropy fraudulent scenarios for stress-testing boundaries, uses Gumbel-Softmax to handle discrete fields like Merchant Category Codes, and triggers expensive SHAP explanations only for high-uncertainty cases, giving a human-in-the-loop workflow tuned for zero-day attacks and GDPR explainability. ([arXiv: Fraud Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13237))  
- A zero-shot benchmark of LLMs on Solidity smart contracts (400 balanced contracts) finds that zero-shot Chain-of-Thought and Tree-of-Thought prompting substantially raise recall for binary vulnerability detection (often to ≈95–99%) at the cost of precision (more false positives), and that for error classification, Claude 3 Opus with ToT achieves the best weighted F1 (90.8), suggesting structured reasoning prompts are valuable for coverage but need calibration for high-precision triage. ([arXiv: Solidity Error Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13239))  
- The AI Security Institute and others are starting to evaluate frontier models in cyber ranges for autonomous attack capability, underscoring policy concern that increasingly agentic models could be misused for offensive security tasks. ([Latent.Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-nvidia-gtc-jensen-goes-hard))  

## Human perceptions of AI responsibility

- Experimental work on causal attribution in AI-related harm scenarios finds that when an AI has moderate or high agency (e.g., choosing means or both goals and means), participants assign greater causal responsibility to the AI, but when AI agency is low they see the human as more causal despite temporal distance; reversing roles shows humans are generally judged more causal than AIs for the same action, developers are seen as highly causal despite being remote in the causal chain, and when an AI is decomposed into an LLM plus an “agentic” wrapper, the agentic component is judged more causal. ([arXiv: Causality Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13236))  

## Classical ML and time-series/narrative structure

- RFX‑Fuse resurrects and extends Breiman & Cutler’s original vision of Random Forest as a unified ML engine: a single GPU/CPU-accelerated forest now provides classification, regression, unsupervised learning, proximity-based similarity, prototypes, outlier detection, missing-value imputation, and four types of importance (overall/local feature importance and overall/local “proximity importance” that explains similarity), often replacing pipelines that currently rely on XGBoost+FAISS+SHAP+Isolation Forest. ([arXiv: RFX-Fuse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13234))  
- Feature-Based Trajectory Clustering (FBTC) tackles longitudinal data by mapping each trajectory to a 20-dimensional feature vector capturing interpretable characteristics of its time evolution (e.g., maxima, derivatives, integral-based measures) and then applying spectral clustering to the resulting point cloud, enabling discovery of non-convex clusters in irregularly sampled time series. ([arXiv: FBTC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13254))  
- A narratology-driven study of Chinese online literature adapts Propp’s morphology to modern web fiction, defining 34 narrative functions (including genre-specific ones like “Golden Finger,” “Face-Slapping,” “Transfiguration”), building a human-annotated corpus of 1,000 segments across 100 novels in five genres, and showing that LLM-generated stories display severe structural homogenization because models fail to truly grasp narrative functions and instead rely on rigid, repetitive generation paradigms. ([arXiv: Chinese Narrative Homogeneity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14430))
