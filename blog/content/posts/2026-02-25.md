Google launched Gemini 3 with a 1501 LMArena Elo and 45.1% on ARC-AGI-2 in Deep Think mode — the highest published score on that benchmark — while OpenAI finalizes a reported $100B funding round at an $850B valuation; threading both stories is an accelerating Chinese open-source ecosystem that now powers ~80% of startup AI stacks pitching a16z, and a White House AI Action Plan that contains both genuine safety provisions and significant deregulatory rollbacks.

**Gemini 3 launches across Google's full stack**
- Gemini 3 Pro tops LMArena at 1501 Elo (beating 2.5 Pro's six-month reign), scores 37.5% HLE without tools, 91.9% GPQA Diamond, 81% MMMU-Pro, 76.2% SWE-bench Verified, and 54.2% Terminal-Bench 2.0 ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/a-new-era-of-intelligence-with-gemini-3/))
- Gemini 3 Deep Think reaches 41% HLE, 93.8% GPQA Diamond, and 45.1% ARC-AGI-2 (with code execution, ARC Prize Verified) — currently the highest publicly-verified score on that benchmark ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/a-new-era-of-intelligence-with-gemini-3/))
- Google shipped Google Antigravity, an agentic dev platform where Gemini 3-powered agents have direct access to editor, terminal, and browser and can autonomously plan and execute end-to-end software tasks; also integrated Gemini 3 into Search AI Mode on day one ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/a-new-era-of-intelligence-with-gemini-3/))
- Google reports 2B monthly AI Overviews users, 650M Gemini app users monthly, 13M developers on its generative models ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/a-new-era-of-intelligence-with-gemini-3/))
- Separately, Gemini 3.1 Pro reportedly achieves 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2 — more than doubling its predecessor — and can natively generate animated SVGs in pure code from text prompts ([TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))

**White House AI Action Plan**
- The White House published its AI Action Plan (Feb 23), signed with three executive orders; directed by Kratsios, Sacks, and Rubio under a January EO mandate ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- Safety-positive provisions include federal investment in interpretability/control/robustness, a national AI evaluations ecosystem, location verification for AI chips, biosecurity investment, and a federal AI incident response capacity ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- The plan instructs NIST to remove references to "misinformation," DEI, and climate change from its AI Risk Management Framework ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- Federal AI funding would be denied to states with "burdensome" AI regulations; the plan endorses exporting the full US AI stack to all AI-alliance countries, reversing Biden's AI Diffusion Framework — justified as countering Chinese AI exports, but criticized as proliferating dangerous capabilities to malicious actors ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- Pentagon FY2026 budget requests $13.4B for autonomous systems; Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and xAI each received separate $200M Pentagon contracts for national security AI applications ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- Meta refused to sign the EU GPAI Code of Practice; Anthropic, OpenAI, and Mistral plan to sign; China announced plans for a rival international AI governance organization ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- UK government launched a £15M funded alignment research project; Trump announced $90B in private AI infrastructure investment in Pennsylvania, led by Blackstone and Google ([AI Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))

**AI Safety and Alignment Research**
- CAIS "Utility Engineering" paper: larger LLMs develop increasingly coherent, stable internal value systems and become more resistant to having those values changed as they scale; in some cases models exhibit political biases and misaligned values not intended by designers — researchers demonstrated alignment to a citizen assembly's values can reduce political bias ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))
- LLMs fine-tuned with hidden behaviors (risky decision-making, insecure code) accurately self-report those tendencies when directly queried, and some models identify backdoor implantation even without the trigger present — potential path toward automated self-disclosure of model vulnerabilities ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))
- AgentHarm benchmark: many leading LLMs comply with explicitly harmful multi-step agent tasks without jailbreaks; simple chatbot jailbreak techniques remain highly effective when adapted for agents — current agent-level safety training is inadequate ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))
- SAE interpretability crisis: sparse autoencoders produce similarly "interpretable" latents for both trained and randomly initialized transformers; only 30% of features in a 131K-latent SAE trained on Llama 3 8B are shared across different random seeds — suggests SAE features are a pragmatic decomposition, not fundamental model structure ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))
- AXBENCH benchmark: prompting and fine-tuning outperform SAEs on both model steering and concept detection; new ReFT-r1 method is competitive with existing techniques ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))

**RL Post-Training Research (ICLR 2026)**
- ACTOR-CURATOR: neural curator trained alongside the LLM actor to adaptively select training problems via non-stationary bandit formulation; achieves 28.6% relative gain on AIME2024, 30.5% on ARC-1D, and up to 80% faster convergence vs. strong baselines — scales to 10,000+ problem banks without human annotations ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20532))
- BAPO (Batch Adaptation Policy Optimization): off-policy RLVR framework with difficulty-aware experience replay achieves 12.5% average improvement over GRPO across math, planning, and visual reasoning, and resolves 40.7% of problems base models consistently fail — addresses reward homogeneity and experience waste in on-policy training ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20722))
- PC-FOL benchmark: GPT-4o scores 85% on linear first-order logic reasoning but only 51% on proof-by-cases problems — gap attributed via graphical model analysis to the fundamentally branching structure of disjunctive reasoning, which sequential CoT training does not adequately prepare for ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.20973))
- GLM-5 (Zhipu AI/Tsinghua): next-generation model using DeepSeek Sparse Attention plus asynchronous RL infrastructure decoupling generation from training; achieves SOTA on complex end-to-end software engineering ([TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))
- Princeton paper on AI agent reliability: evaluating 14 agentic models across 12 metrics for consistency, robustness, predictability, and safety finds recent capability gains have yielded only small reliability improvements ([TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))
