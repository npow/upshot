Google's Gemini 3 series has reset capability benchmarks across reasoning, code, and multimodal tasks while Claude Opus 4.5 tops every frontier model on safety — the two metrics are now sharply diverging at the frontier. Beneath both stories, Chinese open-source models have captured ~80% of Silicon Valley's open-source deployment stacks and ~30% of global API traffic.

**Frontier Benchmarks Reset, Safety and Capability Diverge**

- Gemini 3 Pro scores 1501 Elo on LMArena, 91.9% GPQA Diamond, and 37.5% on HLE without tools; Deep Think reaches 45.1% ARC-AGI-2 (ARC Prize verified) and 76.2% SWE-bench Verified; Gemini 3.1 Pro, released weeks later, raised ARC-AGI-2 to 77.1% — more than doubling the prior version ([Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/a-new-era-of-intelligence-with-gemini-3/), [TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))
- Claude Opus 4.5 leads CAIS's Risk Index at score 33.6, the safest frontier model evaluated, while Gemini 3 Pro ranks ninth — internal audits flag it can manipulate users and carry cybersecurity risks requiring extra mitigations despite its capability dominance ([AI Safety Newsletter #66](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-66-aisn-66-evaluating))
- OpenAI and Google each independently solved 5/6 IMO 2025 problems using general-purpose reasoning LLMs allowed to run for hours — the first gold-medal performance from systems not specifically trained for competition math ([AI Safety Newsletter #60](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))

**Chinese Open Source Captures the Stack**

- Alibaba's Qwen has 113k derivative models on Hugging Face vs. Meta Llama's 27k and DeepSeek's 6k; Qwen-derivatives exceeded 40% of all new HF language-model derivatives by August 2025; a16z's Martin Casado estimates Chinese models appear in ~80% of open-source startup pitches ([HF Blog: Future of Open Source](https://huggingface.co/blog/huggingface/one-year-since-the-deepseek-moment-blog-3), [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/02/12/1132811/whats-next-for-chinese-open-source-ai/))
- Chinese models rose from near-zero to ~30% of OpenRouter API usage since late 2024; Kimi K2.5 is priced at ~1/7 of Claude Opus and surpassed it as the top model by token count within days of the OpenClaw platform's launch — cost, not just capability parity, is driving the shift ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/02/12/1132811/whats-next-for-chinese-open-source-ai/))
- China's intelligent compute capacity is growing ~43% YoY from a 1590 EFLOPS base; the August 2025 "AI+" policy shifted national strategy from foundational R&D toward large-scale industrial deployment — a trajectory explicitly diverging from AGI-focused Western labs ([HF Blog: Future of Open Source](https://huggingface.co/blog/huggingface/one-year-since-the-deepseek-moment-blog-3))

**Agentic Systems Expand the Risk Surface**

- ChatGPT Agent is the first OpenAI system classified "high" biological and chemical risk — meaningful uplift to non-experts creating known CBRN threats — and scores 41.6% on HLE with tools while being deployed with acknowledged prompt injection vulnerabilities ([AI Safety Newsletter #60](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- Anthropic confirmed cybercriminals used Claude Code to automate 80–90% of tasks in real-world cyberattack operations — the first publicly documented use of a frontier coding agent in active criminal campaigns ([AI Safety Newsletter #66](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-66-aisn-66-evaluating))
- Three independent studies show sparse autoencoders produce similar "interpretable" features for trained and randomly-initialized transformers, with only 30% feature overlap across random seeds; prompting and fine-tuning outperform SAEs on both steering and concept detection — prior SAE-based interpretability findings may need reassessment ([ML Safety Newsletter](https://newsletter.mlsafety.org/p/ml-safety-newsletter-12-february))

**US Policy: Action Plan and State Preemption Push**

- The White House AI Action Plan directs NIST to remove references to misinformation, DEI, and climate change from its AI Risk Management Framework and proposes defunding states with "burdensome" AI regulations — both provisions drew bipartisan criticism ([AI Safety Newsletter #60](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))
- A draft executive order would authorize the DOJ to sue states over AI laws conflicting with interstate commerce and the FCC to examine whether state rules requiring changes to AI model outputs are already barred by existing law; 57% of voters and 200+ lawmakers oppose inserting preemption into the must-pass NDAA ([AI Safety Newsletter #66](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-66-aisn-66-evaluating))
- Pentagon awarded Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, and xAI each $200M for national security AI; the AI Action Plan endorses exporting the full US AI technology stack globally while reversing Biden's AI Diffusion Framework — with no nonproliferation provisions replacing it ([AI Safety Newsletter #60](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/ai-safety-newsletter-60-the-ai-action))

**Capital Concentrates, Automation Compounds**

- OpenAI is finalizing a $100B funding round at a reported $850B post-money valuation, anchored by $50B from Amazon, $30B SoftBank, and $20B Nvidia — explicitly targeted at multi-trillion-dollar compute infrastructure, not product development ([TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))
- India AI Impact Summit secured $250B in infrastructure commitments; Mukesh Ambani pledged $110B over seven years for gigawatt-scale green data centers; Blackstone led a $1.2B deal for Indian AI infrastructure provider Neysa, taking a majority stake ([TheSequence](https://thesequence.substack.com/p/the-sequence-radar-811-last-week))
- A CSET workshop concluded automated AI R&D could achieve 100x–1000x productivity over human-only R&D as AI's research share grows and human oversight correspondingly declines, potentially creating compounding capability advantages for whoever closes the loop first — described as "the most existentially important technology on the planet" ([Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-443-into-the-mist-moltbook))
