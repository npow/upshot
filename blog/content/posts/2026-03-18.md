## OpenAI small models and agentic pricing

- OpenAI released GPT‑5.4 mini and GPT‑5.4 nano, 400K‑context models optimized for coding, tool use, multimodal understanding, and computer control, with GPT‑5.4 mini reaching 54.4% on SWE‑Bench Pro (vs 57.7% for full GPT‑5.4) and 72.1% on OSWorld‑Verified (vs 75.0%). ## Reasoning, uncertainty, and RL with richer rewards

- Latent Posterior Factors (LPF) introduce a framework that turns VAE latent posteriors into soft likelihood factors for Sum‑Product Networks (LPF‑SPN) or a learned aggregator (LPF‑Learned), enabling calibrated probabilistic reasoning over multiple noisy evidence sources (e.g., text, images) with strong performance on 8 domains including FEVER. ([arXiv – LPF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15670))

- The companion theoretical LPF paper proves seven guarantees—bounded calibration error (ECE ≤ ε + C/√K_eff), Monte Carlo error O(1/√M), non‑vacuous PAC‑Bayes generalization bounds, near‑optimal calibration relative to an information‑theoretic lower bound, robustness under evidence corruption, O(1/√K) calibration scaling with evidence count, and exact epistemic/aleatoric uncertainty decomposition—and validates them empirically with mean accuracy 99.3% and ECE 1.5% across 8 domains. ([arXiv – LPF Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15674))

- Self‑Reflective Program Search for Long Context (SRLM) augments Recursive Language Model‑style programmatic context interaction with three intrinsic uncertainty signals—self‑consistency across samples, reasoning trace length, and verbalized confidence—to choose among candidate “program trajectories,” achieving up to 22% accuracy gains over RLM baselines under the same time budget and avoiding recursion‑induced degradation at shorter context lengths. ([arXiv – SRLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15653))

- SRLM’s analysis finds recursion itself is not the main driver of long‑context performance; instead, uncertainty‑aware trajectory selection yields more robust gains, particularly on semantically intensive tasks where heuristic recursion fails. ([arXiv – SRLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15653))

- Alternating Reinforcement Learning with Rubric Rewards (ARL‑RR) tackles multi‑dimensional rubric feedback (accuracy, completeness, instruction following, context awareness, communication quality) without collapsing it into a fixed linear scalar: it alternates optimization over “meta‑classes” of criteria, plus a search‑based scheduler that picks which meta‑class to emphasize, and consistently outperforms scalarized reward methods on the HealthBench medical dataset across 1.7B–14B Qwen3 models in both performance and training efficiency. ([arXiv – ARL‑RR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15646))

- KDnuggets summarizes production‑grade hallucination mitigation patterns, emphasizing RAG grounding, mandatory citations (“no sources, no answer”), tool‑calling instead of model recall for transactional facts, post‑generation verification (judge models, Chain‑of‑Verification, lexical checks), quote‑heavy responses, calibrated uncertainty with safe failure paths, and continuous evaluation/monitoring over one‑off prompt tweaks. ([KDnuggets](https://www.kdnuggets.com/7-ways-to-reduce-hallucinations-in-production-llms))

## Architectures, tokenization, and forecasting

- A pediatric EHR transformer study systematically varies tokenization strategies (event encoding, time encoding, workflow annotations) and finds that joint event encoding (binding codes and attributes into a single token) and positional time encodings calibrated to time intervals outperform alternatives on 73/74 and 71/74 predictive tasks respectively, while reducing pretraining FLOPs by 39.5% and 9.6%. ([arXiv – Tokenization Tradeoffs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15644))

- The EHR work attributes joint encoding’s advantage to “local binding efficiency”: combining code‑attribute pairs into single tokens avoids forcing the model to learn their associations from relatively small EHR corpora, and this benefit generalizes across institutions despite vocabulary mismatches, whereas temporal and workflow benefits are more site‑specific. ([arXiv – Tokenization Tradeoffs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15644))

- XLinear proposes a time‑series forecaster that keeps an MLP backbone but adds an Enhanced Frequency Attention (EFA) block on trend components and a CrossFilter block on seasonal/noisy components; leveraging FFT‑based frequency operations and GELU‑combined filters, it achieves state‑of‑the‑art accuracy on 8 long‑range forecasting benchmarks while retaining the robustness and lightweight profile of MLP models. ([arXiv – XLinear](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15645))

- Latent Space’s recap highlights Moonshot’s Attention Residuals (“vertical attention” across depth) and Mamba‑3 as signs that labs are experimenting beyond pure transformers: Attention Residuals let each layer query prior layer states with minimal latency hit (since #layers ≪ sequence length), while Mamba‑3, a state‑space/linear hybrid, claims best‑in‑class performance among linear models and fastest prefill+decode at 1.5B parameters, targeting inference‑heavy RL and long‑rollout settings. ([Latent Space – AINews](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-claude-cowork-dispatch-anthropics))

## Knowledge graphs, morphology, and symbolic reasoning

- HYQNET introduces a hyperbolic neural‑symbolic model for first‑order logic query answering on knowledge graphs: it decomposes queries into fuzzy‑set operations (projection, conjunction, disjunction, negation), performs relation projections via a hyperbolic GNN with learned curvature, and outperforms Euclidean baselines on three standard FOL query datasets by better capturing hierarchical query trees. ([arXiv – HYQNET](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15633))

- “Morphemes Without Borders” evaluates Arabic tokenizers and LLMs on root‑pattern morphology using gold segmentations and a new test set of real and nonce roots, finding that morphological alignment of tokenizers (including MorphBPE‑style approaches) is neither necessary nor sufficient for productive morphological generation—GPT‑4, with high fertility and low morphological alignment on Arabic, is among the best morphological generators. ([arXiv – Morphemes Without Borders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15773))

- The Arabic study’s result challenges assumptions that morphology‑aware tokenization will automatically improve downstream performance in non‑concatenative languages and suggests that LLMs may rely heavily on memorization and contextual cues rather than explicit root‑pattern decomposition. ([arXiv – Morphemes Without Borders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.15773))
